# Mathematical Modelling of Defensive Pressure in Football

*PhD Dissertation. July 2026*

# Summary

The growing availability of data in professional football, driven by event-based systems and player-tracking technologies, has opened new possibilities for the objective analysis of performance. However, the characterisation of defensive behaviour has historically been limited by the scarcity of metrics capable of describing defence in a coherent manner, allowing aggregation across players and teams, and incorporating sensitivity to the spatial and temporal dimensions of the game. In this context, the present doctoral thesis has as its transversal axis the characterisation of defensive behaviour in football, addressed from different methodological perspectives and analytical scales, with the aim of developing interpretable, operational, and mathematically grounded metrics.

This thesis provides a coherent and progressive framework for the characterisation of defensive behaviour in professional football, contributing to the development of mathematical and scientific tools that support the analysis of defensive performance in real-world contexts such as scouting and decision-making processes.
